:: About Garrett ::

What's up, I'm Garrett. Bassist, record collector, and unapologetic pony fan. This page is my little slice of the internet to talk about all of it.

The Bass

Bass is where it's at. I love locking in with the drums and holding the whole song together from the bottom end. Whether it's a simple root-note groove or a busier melodic line, the bass is the glue — and it's the most fun seat in the band, no contest.

The Band

I play bass in Nebuladrag. We've been writing, recording, and playing shows, and you can find all of that over at our official site:

» nebuladrag.com «

The Records

When I'm not playing, I'm usually digging through record bins. There's nothing like finding a great pressing you weren't looking for. Check out my collection page to see what's currently on the shelf.

The Ponies

Yes, I like ponies. No, I won't be taking questions at this time. Head over to the Ponies page if you want to know more.
